# Project Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the planned capabilities and improvements for the Smart Personal Finance Analyzer platform.

---

## 🚀 Released: v1.0.0
- **Multi-Platform Experience Platform (MPEP)**: Web (PWA), Android (Compose), iOS (SwiftUI), and Desktop (Tauri) shells fully integrated.
- **Shared packages architecture**: Reusable types, models, validation, configurations, and sync SDK modules.
- **India-First Defaults**: Automated Indian formatting (Lakh/Crore), UPI payments terminology, financial year bounds (1 April - 31 March), and local tax investment concepts (PPF, EPF, NPS, SIPs).

---

## 🎯 Short-Term (Q3 2026)
- **Extensible Country Configuration Packs**: Out-of-the-box support templates for additional regions (US, UK, Germany, Singapore).
- **Advanced OCR 3.0 Processing**: Machine-learning models to parse and classify bank statements automatically.
- **Observability dashboard**: Native OpenTelemetry collector integrations.

---

## 🔮 Long-Term (2027+)
- **Decentralized offline sync**: Peer-to-peer transaction synchronization for household split ledgers.
- **Interactive Financial Twin**: Sandboxed Monte-Carlo cashflow simulation playgrounds.
