# Platform Maintenance & Continuous Improvement Manual

## 1. Quality Drills Cadence
- **Security Scans**: Weekly automated dependency security checkup.
- **Disaster Recovery (DR) Drills**: Simulated database restore drills executed once every quarter.
- **Accessibility Reviews**: Bi-monthly audits checking tap targets and focus loops.

## 2. Release Calendar & Support Lifecycles
- Releases are scheduled on Tuesday mornings during off-peak hours (02:00 UTC).
- Critical vulnerabilities require hotfixes within 12 hours of discovery.
