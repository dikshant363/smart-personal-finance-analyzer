# Data Governance Policy

## 1. Retention Policy
- Transaction registers are stored indefinitely while the account is active.
- Document image assets (PDF, JPEG statements) are purged 90 days after extraction confirmation unless opted-in for long-term storage.

## 2. Backup & Recovery
- Daily incremental backups, weekly full snapshots.
- Backup snapshots must be encrypted at rest.

## 3. Privacy Policy
- Financial details are private. Workspace sharing relies on explicit granular permission settings.
- Export options must download standard encrypted JSON/CSV archives directly to the user.
