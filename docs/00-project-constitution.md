## 01. Vision
To build the world's most intelligent, resilient, accessible, and privacy-first personal finance platform. We empower individuals and families to take absolute control of their financial destiny by transforming raw transactional records into actionable, explainable, and automated pathways toward long-term wealth, debt freedom, and financial peace.

## 02. Mission
- **Democratize Intelligence**: Deliver institutional-grade financial analytics and predictions (e.g., Monte Carlo simulations, cash-flow twins, debt waterfall modeling) to everyday users.
- **Enforce Absolute Privacy**: Keep user data locked down, masked, and local-first wherever possible. Zero unencrypted financial exposures.
- **Ensure Continuous Reliability**: Deliver a platform that functions seamlessly across networks, offline states, and multiple currencies with zero database corruption or balance fabrication.

## 03. Product Goals
- **Real-Time Data Integration**: Synchronize multiple financial accounts, transaction ledgers, liabilities, and investment portfolios instantly.
- **Explainable Analytics**: Every prediction or budget recommendation made by the system must be fully explainable and auditable by the user. No "black-box" decision processes.
- **Zero-Friction UX**: A highly accessible (WCAG AA), responsive, command-centric interface that responds instantly to user keyboard actions and voice cues.

## 04. Scope
- **Core Platform**: Web application featuring dashboards, transaction histories, goal settings, emergency funds trackers, recurring schedules, and net-worth calculations.
- **Intelligence Layer**: Dynamic analytics engines, cash-flow simulation twins, debt optimization models, and AI copilot interactions.
- **Enterprise Foundation**: High performance caching, database read replicas, circuit breaker policies, GDPR/HIPAA-compliant personal field masking, and auditing frameworks.

## 05. Roadmap
- **Phase 1-4**: Basic financial ledger, net-worth mapping, offline caching, and multi-currency displays. (Completed)
- **Phase 5-8**: Platform hardening, explainability engines, digital twins, and banking integrations. (Completed)
- **Phase 9**: Intelligent operations, event streaming, prompt safety filters, and production launch preparation. (Completed)
- **Phase 10 (LTS)**: Continuous maintenance, security auditing, and cloud-native vertical scaling.
